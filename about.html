Who We Are

Elismos International Limited

Elismos International Ltd. is a fully indigenous private electrical services, procurement, and supply company. We were founded in 2011 and are based at No 16 Nwanwa Street, Port Harcourt, River State. Our strategic location and community-focused approach enable us to serve clients efficiently across the region.

We offer a comprehensive portfolio of services, seamlessly integrating traditional electrical work with cutting-edge renewable energy solutions. Our team of skilled professionals has extensive experience in everything from industrial instrumentation to large-scale solar power system deployment, ensuring we can be your single, reliable partner for all your energy and electrical needs.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

Our mission is to power a brighter, more sustainable future by providing exceptional electrical services and renewable energy solutions. We strive to be a trusted partner to our clients, ensuring every project is completed with the highest standards of quality, safety, and efficiency.

We believe that access to reliable and safe power is a fundamental pillar of progress. Our mission is not just about installing wires and panels; it's about building a foundation for growth, fostering innovation, and contributing to the well-being of the communities we serve.

Where We're Going

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the leading electrical and renewable energy solutions provider in the region, recognized for our unwavering commitment to quality, integrity, and client satisfaction.

We aspire to be innovators in our field, setting new standards for efficiency and sustainability.
